# Synthetic restoration operations handbook

Training and system-evaluation material only. These are fictional internal procedures, separate from policy coverage.

## O1 Contractor dispatch

Confirm the required specialty, service area, credentials, safety conditions and arrival availability. Create a proposed scope and present the exact proposal for authorized human approval. Dispatch only the approved version. Use a stable operation key so retries cannot create duplicate jobs.

## O2 SLA and escalation

The contractor must acknowledge a routine assignment within four hours. For an urgent assignment, require acknowledgement within one hour and arrival within four hours. If a deadline passes without confirmation, record the missed milestone and escalate to the duty supervisor. Preserve the original deadline in the audit trail.

## O3 Damage assessment

Ask when damage was first observed, which areas and materials were affected, and whether conditions are still changing. Record source and uncertainty for all observations. Separate visible evidence from inferred cause, hidden damage and customer-confirmed facts. Request a qualified inspection when a photograph cannot establish the condition.

## O4 Safety handoff

Flag an immediate hazard for human assistance and prioritize safety messaging before routine intake. Keep the customer's own statement separate from model observations. Eligibility, coverage decisions and contractor dispatch require their respective authority checks.
