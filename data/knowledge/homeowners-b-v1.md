# Synthetic Birch homeowners form B

Training and system-evaluation material only. This document is fictional and is not an insurance contract. Policy SYN-456, form version v1.

## B1 Dwelling coverage

Only the listed dwelling is eligible. The synthetic dwelling limit is $180,000. Detached structures and personal property are excluded from this form unless scheduled in writing.

## B2 Appliance water exclusion

Sudden and accidental discharge from a dishwasher, washing machine or other household appliance is excluded, including resulting damage to floors, walls and cabinetry. A separately listed indoor supply pipe break may be potentially covered. Flood and long-term seepage remain excluded.

## B3 Fire and smoke

Damage from an accidental fire is potentially covered. Separate smoke damage from a neighboring property is potentially covered only when smoke entered the dwelling during the documented fire event.

## B4 Wind restriction

Roof wear and wind damage to loose exterior objects are excluded. Sudden wind damage to eligible attached fixtures carries a maximum of $7,500 per occurrence.

## B5 Deductible

All eligible property losses apply a $2,000 deductible per occurrence. No deductible is assessed for an excluded loss.
