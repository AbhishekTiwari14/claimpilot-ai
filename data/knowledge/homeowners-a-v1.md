# Synthetic Cedar homeowners form A

Training and system-evaluation material only. This document is fictional and is not an insurance contract. Policy SYN-123, form version v1.

## A1 Homeowners property coverage

The dwelling, attached fixtures and permanent improvements are eligible property. Coverage applies only to listed events during the policy term, subject to the applicable exclusions and limits. Detached structures have a separate synthetic limit of $20,000. Personal property is covered only when listed in the declarations.

## A2 Sudden indoor water discharge

Direct physical damage to the dwelling caused by sudden and accidental discharge from an indoor plumbing or household appliance system is potentially covered. The failed pipe or appliance itself is excluded. This clause does not extend to outdoor flooding or water entering from below ground.

## A3 Water exclusions

Loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage over 14 or more consecutive days is excluded. Surface water, river flooding, groundwater and water backing up through a sewer are excluded under this form. Drying expenses are considered only when tied to an otherwise covered event.

## A4 Fire and smoke

Direct damage from an accidental dwelling fire or its resulting smoke is potentially covered. Smoke from normal cooking, routine industrial operations or repeated long-term exposure is excluded. Investigators must distinguish soot deposition from prior surface staining before determining applicability.

## A5 Storm and wind

Sudden direct wind damage to a dwelling roof is potentially covered. Interior rain damage is potentially covered only when the same storm first creates an opening in the roof or wall. Wear, deterioration and pre-existing openings are excluded. Document weather timing and the opening before relying on this clause.

## A6 Deductible and limits

The standard property deductible is $1,000 per covered occurrence. A separate $2,500 wind deductible replaces the standard deductible for a covered wind loss. The synthetic dwelling limit is $250,000. A deductible is applied after determining the eligible covered loss.
