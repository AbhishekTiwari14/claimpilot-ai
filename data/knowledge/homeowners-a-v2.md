# Synthetic Cedar homeowners form A revision

Training and system-evaluation material only. This document is fictional and is not an insurance contract. Policy SYN-123, form version v2.

## V2 Water discharge

Sudden indoor water escape is potentially covered only when directly caused by a broken water supply pipe. Damage caused by an appliance malfunction is excluded.

## V2 Schedule deductible

The dwelling deductible for covered accidental fire damage is $1,500 per occurrence.

## V2 Fire addendum deductible

The dwelling deductible for covered accidental fire damage is $3,000 per occurrence. The supplied synthetic addendum does not identify whether it replaces the schedule.

## V2 Wind limit

The maximum payout for eligible wind damage is $10,000, subject to assessment against the dwelling limit.
