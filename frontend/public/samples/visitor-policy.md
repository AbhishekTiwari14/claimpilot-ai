# Synthetic Visitor Home Policy

This policy is fictional and supplied solely for the ClaimPilot demonstration. It is not an insurance contract.

## Coverage for sudden appliance leaks

Direct physical damage to insured dwelling surfaces caused by a sudden, accidental household appliance leak is potentially covered. This can include kitchen flooring damaged by a dishwasher that releases water unexpectedly.

## Exclusion for long-term seepage

Continuous or repeated seepage over 14 or more consecutive days is excluded. The failed appliance itself is also excluded from reimbursement.

## Deductible

The synthetic dwelling deductible is $1,250 for each covered occurrence.
